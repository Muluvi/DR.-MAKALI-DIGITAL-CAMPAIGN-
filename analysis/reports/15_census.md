## Checks

- The package reproduces Kitui's published census figures: 1,136,187 people, 42.9% phone ownership, 13.6% internet use.
- Sub-county populations, adults and households each sum exactly to the county.
- All 18 census sub-county names match the polygon file's.


## Limits

- 2019 figures. Phone and internet use have moved since: Kitui's county internet rate was 26.2% in 2023/24 against 13.6% in 2019. Sub-county rates for 2023/24 are not published.
- The two boundary files were digitised separately; slivers under 1% of a ward are dropped.
- No literacy table is in the 2019 census volumes the package carries (Vols. I–IV). literacy.county stays open.
