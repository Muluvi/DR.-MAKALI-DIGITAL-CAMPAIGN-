## What the pack already establishes

The pack has no rival digital metrics at all — followers, posting frequency, top content and ad activity are all listed as data gaps (pack gaps 4 and 5). What it does establish is non-digital and still matters for the benchmark:

- **Kasalu won the 2022 Woman Rep race with 201,899 votes** — more than the winning gubernatorial tally of 198,004. She has already demonstrated countywide vote-getting capacity above the benchmark. The total is a media report of the declaration; IEBC Form 39C is the record to hold.
- **Kasalu is visibly campaigning on the ground**: a #ZiaraMashinani grassroots tour and a visibility push in Kitui East [S28, S29].
- **Ngilu has a reported media asset.** Mbaitu FM is reported as linked to her [S38], and Athiani FM as linked to the party leader. Both are T3 and carry verify. Media planning has to assume neither station is neutral.
- **Mulu's own baseline is ~15,000 Facebook followers and 745 posts**, read off a screenshot and carrying verify. Until rival counts exist, there is no benchmark to read it against.
