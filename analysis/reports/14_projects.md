## Limits

- The scorecard is Tier 1 as a record of what the county claims, not evidence that the claims are true.
- The place text was extracted from a PDF table. Where a cell wrapped, the ward is cut off; a cut-off ward is matched only when the words left name one ward in the constituency.
- Ward totals cover single-ward projects only. Multi-ward, conflict, constituency and county-wide projects are totalled separately and never spread across wards.
- Totals reconcile to the scorecard's 401 rows and KSh 1,363,953,262.65 (lib/data/figures.ts scorecard.itemised.ksh).
