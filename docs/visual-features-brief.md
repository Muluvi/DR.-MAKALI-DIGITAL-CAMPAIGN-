# The Ultimate Comprehensive List of Visual Features for a Website

This list consolidates every visual feature, animation, interaction, and
enhancement imaginable for modern websites, incorporating all previously
mentioned items plus additional niche and emerging techniques.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Entrance & Reveal (Page Load / Hero)

-   Fade in --- element gradually appears
-   Slide in --- from up, down, left, right, or diagonal
-   Scale up / pop in --- grows from small to full size with overshoot
-   Scale down / settle in --- starts oversized and shrinks to fit
-   Blur-to-sharp --- element begins blurred and focuses
-   Clip-path wipe --- reveal through a geometric shape (linear,
    circular, diagonal)
-   Mask reveal --- uses an SVG or CSS mask to progressively show
    content
-   Curtain / shutter open --- panels slide apart like stage curtains
-   Iris open --- circular or polygonal aperture expands to reveal
    content
-   Flip in on an axis --- rotates in around X or Y axis
-   Rotate in --- spins from a rotated state to upright
-   Unfold / origami expand --- complex folding animation
-   Stagger cascade across siblings --- sequential delay for each
    sibling
-   Ripple stagger from a focal point --- delays propagate outward from
    a point
-   Text reveal by line, word, or character --- masked or animated text
    segments
-   Typewriter effect --- characters appear one by one
-   SVG path draw --- strokes animate drawing themselves
-   Stroke-to-fill --- outline transitions into filled shape
-   Number count-up --- counters animate from zero to target
-   Skeleton resolving into real content --- placeholders fade out as
    data loads
-   Progressive image load --- blur-up or low-quality image placeholder
    gradually sharpens
-   Blur-In Animation --- begins blurred and sharpens over time
-   Ken Burns Effect --- slow zoom and pan on images
-   Particle overlay --- floating interactive particles react to cursor
-   High-resolution imagery --- crisp images served via CDN for polished
    look
-   Split-screen hero animation --- content slides in from left and
    right simultaneously
-   Fade-in sequence --- multiple elements fade in with timed delays
-   Scale-up animation --- hero elements grow from small to full
-   Curtain reveal --- panels slide away to reveal content underneath
-   Liquid / morphing background --- animated gradient or shape morphs
    behind hero
-   Parallax hero --- background and foreground move at different speeds
-   Video background with overlay --- autoplay video with
    gradient/pattern overlay
-   SVG line drawing animation --- vector illustrations draw themselves
    line by line
-   Glitch effect --- brief digital glitch for cyberpunk/tech aesthetic
-   Spotlight effect --- moving light source illuminates parts of hero
-   Text bounce on load --- playful bouncing header
-   Logo animation --- logo animates on load
-   3D text effect --- depth using shadows/transforms on entry
-   Lottie / Bodymovin animation --- JSON-based vector animations play
    on load
-   View Transitions API --- smooth transition between page states or
    routes
-   Animated preloader --- branded loading sequence before content
    appears

## 2. Exit & Dismissal

-   Fade out --- element gradually disappears
-   Slide out --- in any direction (up, down, left, right)
-   Scale and fade collapse --- shrinks and fades simultaneously
-   Wipe out --- content is clipped away
-   Shrink to origin point --- element collapses to a single point
-   Fly to target --- e.g., add-to-cart arc, element flies to a
    destination
-   Disintegrate / particle dissolve --- element breaks into particles
-   Reverse stagger --- siblings exit in reverse order of entrance
-   Modal exit animations --- scale, fade, or slide out with backdrop
-   Drawer / sheet exit --- slides out to edge
-   Toast / snackbar exit --- fades or slides away
-   Roll out --- element rolls off screen
-   Light speed out --- fast exit with motion blur
-   Bounce out --- bounces away
-   Flip out --- flips off screen

-   Color shift on scroll --- background or text colors change with

-   Scroll-triggered counter reset --- counters reset and recount each

-   Scroll velocity detection --- animation speed reacts to scroll
