# Motion pass, November 2026

The owner asked for motion to be used extensively, wherever it is appropriate. This records what the
site had, what it lacked, what was added, and how it is checked.

## What the site had

- **The motion system:**
  - A token layer (`lib/motion.ts`: durations, easing curves, springs).
  - A CSS-only draw-in for register figures. `components/register/RegisterMotion.tsx` arms a figure below
    the fold, and `app/chart-kit.css` draws it once as it enters view.
- **Kinds that already drew in:** bars, stacks, waterfalls, slopes, timelines, steps, decision trees,
  dumbbells, icon grids, Pareto charts, bubbles, gauges, networks, the house, the balance and the spine.
- **About 30 hand-built panels** animated with `motion/react`.
- **A shared page fade.** A cross-document view transition fades between pages.

## What it lacked

Measured by chart type across the 91 register figures, and by kind across the 119 content visuals.

| Surface | Uses | Motion before |
|---|---:|---|
| Register matrix | 31 | none |
| Register cards | 25 | none |
| Register heatmap, risk grid, calendar, phone mock-up | 5 | none |
| Content checklists | 38 | none |
| Content cards | 31 | none |
| Content stats | 15 | none |
| Content timelines and steppers | 14 | none |
| Content contrast and in/out, bars, donuts, weeks, tables, panels | 14 | none |
| Every fold ("Show the details", "Read the full text", tier chips, panels) | site-wide | snapped open |
| Workstream tabs | 1 bar, 4 panels | panel swapped instantly; the highlight jumped |

The matrix and card figures are the two most common figures on the site. They include most of the
41 figures migrated from the legacy system in the consolidation follow-up.

## What was added

Every kind's motion says something about what it shows. All of it plays once, and only for a visual
that was still below the fold. All of it is CSS (`app/motion-draw.css`), keyed to the same
`.is-pending` / `.is-drawn` classes the register already used. `RegisterMotion` now arms content
visuals (`.vz`) as well as figures (`.rf`).

| Kind | What the reader sees | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Matrix (figures and content tables) | The header settles, then rows arrive top to bottom | The order to read it in |
| Cards (figures and content) | Each card in turn | The same |
| Heatmap | Cells arrive by value, cool first | Where the high values are |
| Risk grid | The grid, then each risk pin lands in its cell | Which cell each risk is in |
| Calendar, week | Monday to Sunday | The week's order |
| Phone mock-up | The screen fills field by field, then each numbered problem is pinned | Where each problem sits |
| Checklist | Each ring settles and its tick draws, item by item | A list being ticked off |
| Stepper | Each number, then its words | Sequence |
| Timeline | The line draws down through the dates; each date appears as the line reaches it | Time passing |
| Stats | Each value settles on its number, the label after it | The number first |
| Bars | Each bar grows from the baseline | Magnitude from zero |
| Donut | Segments fan in clockwise from twelve o'clock, in the legend's order | Part to whole |
| Contrast, in/out | The two sides come from opposite sides | Opposition |
| Hypothesis, Q&A | Panels in the argument's order: the attack before the answer | The argument's order |
| A fold a reader opens | The words come down out of the control | Where they came from |
| Workstream tabs | One highlight slides to the chosen tab; the panel arrives from the side its tab sits on | Which way the reader moved |

**Long sets** cap their stagger, so the last item waits about a second at most.

**Folds.** `FoldBody` marks a fold only when it opens after mounting: a tap, find in page or a deep
link. A fold rendered open never animates.

**Workstream tabs.** The first tab choice, made as the page loads, does not animate.

## What does not move, and why

- **Prose and headings.** `lib/motion.ts` forbids a generic reveal on them, and that rule stands.
- **A visual already on screen.** Animating what the reader is looking at would redraw it under
  their eyes.
- **Without JavaScript, on a low-end device (4 cores or fewer, 2 GB or less) and with Save-Data on.**
  `html.motion-ok` is never set, so every visual is simply drawn.
- **Reduced motion.** There is no travel and no stagger. A visual fades in once; folds and tab
  panels just appear; the tab highlight moves without travel.
- **Print.** Every rule is screen-only. `RegisterMotion` also draws everything on `beforeprint`.

## Checks

`scripts/consolidation/motion-check.mjs` runs against a running copy of the site. It emulates an
eight-core device, because `RegisterMotion` rightly leaves motion off on four cores or fewer.

0. **Static.** `app/motion-draw.css` animates only compositor properties: opacity, translate, scale,
   rotate, transform and stroke-dashoffset. Motion therefore cannot shift the layout around it.
1. **Every chapter at 390px, scrolled a screen at a time.**
   - At no step is a visual still pending while it sits in view.
   - Any visual the scroll passed while its section was streaming in draws when scrolled back to.
   - Every moved mark settles at full opacity with no leftover offset.
   - Layout shift is reported with motion on and off. Some chapters shift as their sections stream
     in, with or without motion.
2. **Reduced motion.** Nothing is stuck, unsettled or staggered.
3. **JavaScript off.** Nothing is ever pending.
4. **Print.** Every mark is at full opacity.
5. **Folds and tabs.**
   - A tapped fold plays once and clears its mark.
   - Nothing moves on the workstream page's load.
   - A later tab's panel arrives from the right, and an earlier tab's from the left.
   - The mark clears, and there is one highlight.
6. **Frame timing.** Reported, not enforced, at 4x CPU slowdown.
