## Judgement calls

- **Women: a segment across the six, not a seventh tab.** Four figures and the segmentation matrix describe "six segments" with sizing and channels. A seventh tab would contradict all of them. So the women's segment is set out at the head of 3.4.2 as one that cuts across the six. It has its size (52% of the register), its evidence, its lead pillar, its channels and its proof, as the note recommends. Annex C already assigned a message to women's cooperatives and table-banking groups; this makes them a segment.
- **Security and resource conflict is a paragraph under 3.2.4, not a seventh theme card.** The pillar crosswalk counts six themes, and pillar consolidation is an open owner decision. The evidence is in place for the owner to promote it.
- **The 61% is held, not substituted.** The note says to check the measure first, and the CIDP page is not in hand.
- **The scorecard is Tier 1 only as a record of what the county claims.** Every place it is used says so. Q1–Q9 are put as questions to test, never as accusations. During the nomination window, messaging uses only the verification half of the slogan contrast, because the administration is Wiper.
- **Millions to one decimal.** The scorecard's amounts are printed as the source gives them ("KSh 254.9m"). A new `|m1` token modifier does this; `|m` rounds to whole millions.
- **The 2.12 addition is in the card's details.** Added to the visible "what supports it" panel, it made a paragraph over 60 words.

## How the change is checked

The declutter's conservation check ran from `4bfc078`. Its verdict at the merge (`8f10cb9`) is recorded in `docs/declutter/REPORT.md`.

From this pass on, `npm run check:content` runs the same checks (fences, word conservation, conflict markers) from `8f10cb9` against `ledger.json`. A logged addition or replacement counts as part of its section's base, and the words it brings are debited from that section's balance, so an unlogged loss still fails. Balance: −0.03%. All 27 conflict markers are in place.

## Figures that do not reconcile

- **Typhoid.** The note gives a 52.2% rise in typhoid, but its own counts (1,696 to 2,564) give 51.2%. The text cites the two counts and no percentage.
- **Water sources.** "450 of 630 (72%)" working is 71.4% on the counts. The text uses the counts and the share not working, 28.6%, which it calls "more than a quarter".

## Not settled by the pack (for the owner)

- **The 2027 field.** The nomination procedure, rivals' records and the 2026 register are not in the pack. The scorecard names Deputy Governor Augustine Wambua Kanani and the county executive; whether any of them is a 2027 contender should be checked before 2.6 is final.
- **Green grams.** The annual green-gram output behind the KSh 85/kg floor-price costing is still unsourced: the long rains give only part of the year.
- **"17.4% completed secondary"** is still not yet tiered.
- **Ward-level spending.** Ward-level development spending for the whole budget still needs the Controller of Budget's county reports; the scorecard covers a fraction.
- **Whether any scorecard project exists as described.** That is the tracker's job.
- **History not yet used.** The 2015 Statistical Abstract's history (NG-CDF allocations, the 2014 KDHS baseline, 2014/15 absorption) is noted in the source note. Only the NG-CDF allocations are entered as tokens; none is used in the text yet.
- **Not used.** Some pack figures are entered as tokens but not used in the text yet: women's decisions on cash and purchases, literacy, skilled birth attendance, ages 15–24, and the minimum acceptable diet.
