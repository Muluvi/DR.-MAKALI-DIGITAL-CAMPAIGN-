
Kitui's most urgent need is water: it has the lowest share of any county in Kenya with at least a basic drinking-water service, **{{water.basic.kdhs}}%** (KDHS 2022). Behind it come food and drought, health, roads, jobs for the young, the cost of living, how county money is spent, and the unresolved Mui Basin coalfield. Each concern below carries its evidence against the national figure where one is published, its source and year, and what it means for the campaign. The themes the campaign argues from this evidence are in [One claim, three pillars, six themes](#message-one-claim-three-pillars-six-themes).

## Mui Basin coal and displaced communities

The extensive coal deposits in the Mui Basin, straddling Kitui East and Kitui South, have remained undeveloped for over a decade due to unresolved legal challenges under **Petition 12 of 2014**. The standstill leaves communities across the affected wards uncertain whether they will be displaced. The number of concessions and the number of people who could be displaced are **named data gaps**.

**What it means for the campaign.** Mineral wealth managed for local benefit is a promise to make in Kitui East and Kitui South, and only with the court record and the displacement figures in hand.
