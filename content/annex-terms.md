The abbreviations and terms of art this proposal uses, each defined once in plain words. Where a term names a document, the definition says who issues it.

<!-- keep -->
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ADP** | Annual Development Plan. The county's one-year plan, drawn from the CIDP, that names the projects and programmes for the coming financial year. Prepared by the county executive. |
| **CFSP** | County Fiscal Strategy Paper. The county treasury's annual paper that sets the resource envelope and the ceilings for each sector in the next budget. Tabled before the county assembly. |
| **CIDP** | County Integrated Development Plan. The county's five-year development plan, required by the County Governments Act 2012, against which the ADP and the budget are meant to be set. |
| **ECVR** | Enhanced Continuous Voter Registration. The IEBC's registration drives held in phases ahead of the 2027 general election, on top of the continuous registration it runs at its offices. |
| **Equitable share** | The part of nationally raised revenue that is shared among the 47 counties by the formula the Commission on Revenue Allocation recommends and Parliament approves. It is the largest single item in a county's resource envelope. |
| **GOTV** | Get out the vote. The final phase of a campaign, in which effort moves from persuading voters to making sure supporters actually turn out on polling day. |
| **IEBC Forms 34 to 39** | The statutory results forms, one series per elective office: 34 for president, 35 for member of the National Assembly, 36 for member of the county assembly, 37 for governor, 38 for senator and 39 for woman representative. The A form records the count at a polling station; the B and C forms record the tallies at constituency and county level. |
| **NG-CDF** | National Government Constituencies Development Fund. The national fund, set up under the NG-CDF Act 2015, that finances projects such as classrooms and bursaries in each constituency, overseen by the area's member of Parliament. |
| **Own-source revenue** | The revenue a county raises itself, from fees, charges, licences and local levies, as distinct from transfers from the national government. |
| **USSD** | Unstructured Supplementary Service Data. The short-code menus (dialled as *code#) that work on any mobile phone, including basic phones without data, and on every network. |
