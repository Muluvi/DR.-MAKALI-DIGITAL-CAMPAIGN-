## 5.1 An open seat: the incumbent is term-limited

Governor Malombe served from 2013 to 2017, lost the 2017 election to Charity Ngilu (court-confirmed, Tier 1), and won again in 2022. Article 180(7) of the Constitution of Kenya limits a governor to two terms (Tier 1). He is serving his second, so he cannot stand in 2027, and this proposal plans for an open-seat election throughout. The Auditor-General's and Controller of Budget's reports on the county executive are the documentary record of his administration (Section 3.7).

Malombe's incumbency advantage does not carry into it, the Wiper Patriotic Front's nomination is a contest for an open seat, and factions realign within the party and across independent coalitions. Dr. Mulu stands as the successor with the integrity to lead the county's economic transition, measured against the outgoing administration's record (Section 3.7).

**When.** The Wiper decision is expected in the final quarter of 2026, late October to November on the reported timetable: **a Tier 3, single-source report, not a confirmed party decision.** It is reported to be a party-run countywide selection rather than a competitive primary, and this whole Phase −1 strategy is built on that report (Section 7.4, assumption 1).

**If the report is wrong** — if Wiper runs a competitive primary, a delegate vote, or a hybrid — the phasing in Chapter 14 is revised before more effort is committed against it, and the contingency in Section 5.4 starts. This section exists so that the re-check happens on purpose rather than by accident.

**What the 2022 record shows, and what it does not.** The High Court judgment in *Kilonzo v Wiper Democratic Movement* (2022) records that Wiper's rules allowed nomination by consensus, and by direct or indirect methods, with the National Executive Council deciding the mode; and that for the 2022 Kitui gubernatorial ticket the party's board decided through selected delegates and panels (Tier 1, Kenya Law). That is the 2022 precedent. It is not the 2027 procedure, which rests on the party documents below.

## 5.5 Each rival, and the legal ground to be careful on

```lead
The strategic implication is not to compete for the same countywide presence but to **compete on a dimension she is not occupying**: verifiable fiscal and delivery competence.
```

**Reading the field.** Dr. Kasalu's strength, on the record, is a countywide seat and a 2022 result above the governor's winning tally (Section 5.3; Tier 2 until the IEBC declaration is in hand). The campaign cannot match a sitting Woman Representative's countywide constituency in the time available. The strategic implication is not to compete for the same countywide presence but to **compete on a dimension she is not occupying**: verifiable fiscal and delivery competence, carried by channels that reach the wards where he has never held office. Her documented criticism of the incumbent's equal-ward allocation model also opens a technical policy debate Dr. Mulu is better equipped to win than anyone in the field; Section 10.1 sets out the choice it forces on the campaign's own platform.
