
The working diagnosis is that Dr. Makali Mulu's gap is one of recognition outside Kitui Central, not of credibility. It is a hypothesis, and Week 1 tests it against his own channel data before anything is committed. This chapter states the evidence for it, what could overturn it, what the data cannot yet tell us, and the assumptions the plan rests on.

## What could overturn it

**The case against.** Dr. Kasalu won the 2022 Woman Representative race with **{{result.2022.womanrep.kasalu}} votes**, more than the **{{result.2022.gov.malombe}}** that won the governorship the same year ([Results from 2013 to 2022](#votes-results-from-2013-to-2022)). She has already shown she can win countywide votes above the benchmark. That is not the profile of a race decided by passive name recognition alone.

**The test.** Week 1 reads ninety days of Dr. Mulu's own channels and measures how much of his reach lands in the pool (method: [How the presence audit works](#success-how-the-presence-audit-works)). If his reach is already landing in Mwingi and failing to convert, the problem is the message, not the reach, and the response changes. [When the plan changes](#channels-when-the-plan-changes) sets out in advance what each finding changes, so the audit cannot be read backwards into whatever was already planned.

## What the data cannot yet show

Where data is missing, this plan names the gap rather than filling it:

1.  **Ward-level voter preference:** **not measured, and not sought.** This plan forms strategy from existing records and its own analysis only. The nearest official record is how each ward actually voted: the IEBC's ward and polling-station forms for 2017 and 2022 (see [Results from 2013 to 2022](#votes-results-from-2013-to-2022)), which the analysis uses as soon as they are in hand. Until then it works from where he has held office.
2.  **Ward-by-ward results for the 2013 governor's race:** a **named data gap.** The IEBC's certified ward returns for 2013 are not in the campaign's records.
3.  **Ward-by-ward tallies for the 2017 governor's race:** a **named data gap.** The countywide certified totals are established (Ngilu {{result.2017.gov.ngilu}}, Musila {{result.2017.gov.musila}}, Malombe {{result.2017.gov.malombe}}); the ward breakdown sheets are treated as unverified until they are retrieved from the IEBC.
4.  **Three segment sizes:** the agro-pastoral, the formal-sector and the diaspora voter counts are not published by any official source. [The voters to win](#voters) gives the census context for each; none is estimated, and each stays unsized until an official source publishes it.
