## The public record

Hon. Dr. Benson Makali Mulu, PhD (Economics), CBS, has represented Kitui Central in the National Assembly since 2013. He is a member of the Budget and Appropriations Committee, the committee that scrutinises the national estimates. Whether he has held a leadership position on it is confirmed from the National Assembly's committee membership records and Hansard; until it is, this plan describes him as a member.

He is a specialist in project management, strategic planning and **monitoring and evaluation (M&E)**: a certified consultant in project management and M&E, a member of the Evaluation Society of Kenya, and an **M&E Champion designated by the Ministry of Finance**, after service with the United Nations in financial advisory and project management roles. Under his stewardship, Kitui Central was evaluated the **best constituency in the Eastern region for FY2014/15**, first of 71 constituencies in its peer group. This plan has attributed the evaluation to Ministry of Finance performance audits; the evaluation report itself, and the body that issued it, are to be cited before the line is published.

## Where he is known, and where not


**Where he has held office.** Dr. Mulu has been Member of Parliament for Kitui Central since 2013. His base is Kitui Central ({{con.kitui-central}} voters) and the wards next to it in Kitui West (Matinyani, Kauwi) and Kitui Rural (Kisasi, Mbitini).

**Where he has not.**

1.  **Mwingi North, Mwingi Central and Mwingi West:** **{{bloc.mwingi}} registered voters** (**{{bloc.mwingi.share}}%** of the county register). The hypothesis is that being known as "the Kitui Central MP" limits recall there, against candidates who hold countywide office; Week 1 tests it against his own channel data (see [What the evidence says](#diagnosis-what-the-evidence-says)).
2.  **Kitui South (six wards):** **{{con.kitui-south}} registered voters** (**{{con.kitui-south.share}}%** of the county register, calculated). Remote, separated by long transit corridors, and shaped by its own local leadership.
3.  **Kitui East (outside the pool):** **{{con.kitui-east}} registered voters** (IEBC; particularly Endau/Malalani, Voo/Kyamatu and Mutito/Kaliku). He has not held office here either, but Kitui East is not counted in the pool; it is a direct-contact target, on the party-flow finding in [Where party loyalty will not carry him](#record-where-party-loyalty-will-not-carry-him).

## Where party loyalty will not carry him


**Kitui Central will be fighting its own contest.** Stephen Kilonzo ("Kitundumo") has reportedly entered the Kitui Central MP race, and NLP leader Augustus Muli is weighing a bid there (both become record only when the party nomination lists and the IEBC's gazetted nominations are published, and Mr Muli's party leadership is a matter of the Registrar of Political Parties' register). Dr. Mulu's home constituency, {{con.kitui-central}} voters or {{con.kitui-central.share}}% of the register, will therefore have its own succession fight in the same window as his bid for governor. **Loyalty in the home base cannot be left to look after itself**, and the content weights in [A week of publishing, before and after](#channels-a-week-of-publishing-before-and-after) do not assume it.

**Two constituencies will not carry party flow.** Kitui East returned a **UDA** MP and Kitui South a **Jubilee** MP in 2022 (constituency records; the IEBC's 2022 declarations for both seats are the record to cite). Together they hold **{{east-south}} registered voters, {{east-south.share}}% of the county**. Party structures do not reach those local networks, so reaching them is a direct-contact problem, not a party-mobilisation one. Kitui South sits inside the pool and Kitui East beside it, where he has also never held office, so this adds to the [Where he is known, and where not](#record-where-he-is-known-and-where-not) finding rather than offsetting it.
