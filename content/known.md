## 6.2 Where he is known, and where not

```lead
**Five of the twelve largest wards lie where he has never held office.** The pool is {{pool.share}}% of the entire Kitui County voter register. The pool is structural: it is derived from where he has held office, not from any measure of opinion.
```

**Where he has held office.** Dr. Mulu has been Member of Parliament for Kitui Central since 2013. His base is Kitui Central ({{con.kitui-central}} voters) and the wards next to it in Kitui West (Matinyani, Kauwi) and Kitui Rural (Kisasi, Mbitini).

**Where he has not.**

1.  **Mwingi North, Mwingi Central and Mwingi West:** **{{bloc.mwingi}} registered voters** (**{{bloc.mwingi.share}}%** of the county register). The hypothesis is that being known as "the Kitui Central MP" limits recall there, against candidates who hold countywide office; Week 1 tests it against his own channel data ([what the evidence says](/#diagnosis-sec-7-1)).
2.  **Kitui South (six wards):** **{{con.kitui-south}} registered voters** (**{{con.kitui-south.share}}%** of the county register, calculated). Remote, separated by long transit corridors, and shaped by its own local leadership.
3.  **Kitui East (outside the pool):** **{{con.kitui-east}} registered voters** (IEBC; particularly Endau/Malalani, Voo/Kyamatu and Mutito/Kaliku). He has not held office here either, but Kitui East is not counted in the pool; it is a direct-contact target, on the party-flow finding in [where party loyalty will not carry him](/#known-sec-6-3).

## 6.3 Where party loyalty will not carry him

```lead
**Kitui Central will be fighting its own contest.** **Two constituencies will not carry party flow:** Kitui East returned a UDA MP and Kitui South a Jubilee MP in 2022 (constituency records, unconfirmed).
```

**Kitui Central will be fighting its own contest.** Stephen Kilonzo ("Kitundumo") has reportedly entered the Kitui Central MP race, and NLP leader Augustus Muli is weighing a bid there (unconfirmed and reported respectively; both become record only when the party nomination lists and the IEBC's gazetted nominations are published, and Mr Muli's party leadership is a matter of the Registrar of Political Parties' register). Dr. Mulu's home constituency, {{con.kitui-central}} voters or {{con.kitui-central.share}}% of the register (calculated), will therefore have its own succession fight in the same window as his bid for governor. **Loyalty in the home base cannot be left to look after itself**, and the content weights in [A week of publishing, before and after](/#week-sec-12-6) do not assume it.

**Two constituencies will not carry party flow.** Kitui East returned a **UDA** MP and Kitui South a **Jubilee** MP in 2022 (constituency records, unconfirmed; the IEBC's 2022 declarations for both seats are the record to cite). Together they hold **{{east-south}} registered voters, {{east-south.share}}% of the county** (calculated). Party structures do not reach those local networks, so reaching them is a direct-contact problem, not a party-mobilisation one. Kitui South sits inside the pool and Kitui East beside it, where he has also never held office, so this adds to the [where he is known, and where not](/#known-sec-6-2) finding rather than offsetting it.
