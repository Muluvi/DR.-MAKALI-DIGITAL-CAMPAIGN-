## C.1 The published rounds, and what each one measured

Three published rounds exist, from **two different pollsters**. Each is its own row. They are
deliberately **not drawn as one trend line** and no gap between rounds is computed: different houses
use different samples, screens and question wording, and joining them would manufacture a
trajectory nobody measured.

*Politrack via The County Diary, 12 March 2026; Mizani via Mizani Africa and The County Diary. The
Politrack margins are computed from its published sample on a simple-random assumption, which a
real design would widen.*

**Method differences, and why the rows cannot be compared:**

*   **Neither Mizani round published a sample size**, so no margin of error can be stated and the
    June-to-August change cannot be tested for significance.
*   **The fields differ.** Mizani's June round excluded Ngilu; the August round included her at
    17.0%. The two are not like-for-like.
*   **Undecideds differ tenfold.** Politrack reports 0.6% undecided, Mizani 6.0%, which points to
    different instruments rather than different electorates.
*   **Every round reaches this proposal through a secondary outlet** or a social post. None has
    been read from the pollster's own methodology note.
*   **They measure a nomination preference among whoever was sampled**, countywide. None reports
    ward or sub-county results, which is the level this proposal's analysis works at (Section 3.10).
