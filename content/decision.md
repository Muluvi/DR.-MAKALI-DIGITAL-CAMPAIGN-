#### The ask

Dr. Makali Mulu has the credentials, the experience and the vision to be an
outstanding Governor of Kitui County. He also has something no rival in this
race has: a professional qualification in verifying whether public money
delivered what it promised. Unconfirmed balances, pending bills and low development absorption are what the
Auditor-General and the Controller of Budget report for the county (Annex B.2), and that qualification is currently the campaign's most under-used
asset.

**But the timeline is not the one the planning cycle assumes.** <window-chip></window-chip> Half the register, {{pool.share}}%, lives in the four
constituencies where he has never held office. The working hypothesis is that the gap there is one
of recognition rather than credibility (Section 2.12). If Week 1 confirms it, it is the kind of gap
that closes fastest through disciplined reach into Mwingi and the arid south.

This proposal sets out the layer that turns an active channel into a directed one: an audit of
what the existing accounts are actually doing (Section 4.2.1), a strategy derived from it, a weekly
brief that carries it to the team already publishing, an SMS and USSD layer built for the
{{ict.offline}}% of Kitui residents who are offline, a published ethics charter that turns
careful data practice into a differentiator (Section 5.3.3), a data-protection gate that protects
the candidate from the legal exposure others in this cycle will walk into (Section 5.3.4), and a
public service-delivery tracker that puts his strongest credential to work before a single vote is
cast.

It is built on one premise: **Kitui voters are ready for a leader who
understands economics, respects public money, communicates with clarity and
respect — and is willing to be measured.**

Firefly would like one week and one export. Everything after that is evidence.

**Accessibility is not a service level.** Sign-language interpretation on flagship content, and

#### Level 1 — LEAN
**Purpose:** win the nomination, prove the model, keep the analysis light.
* **Firefly team:** Strategy Director + Analyst; offline-layer operator part-activated
* **Presence audit:** once, at the start
* **Reach reporting:** the 12 decisive wards in Section 2.3 only

* **Creative testing:** monthly cycle
* **Team upskilling:** the written standard, delivered once
* **Offline layer:** SMS to a consented list built organically; **USSD not activated**
* **Trade-offs:** no predictive modelling; no attribution beyond last-click; the recognition
deficit is measured at county level rather than ward level — **which is the one place this campaign
cannot afford to be blind**

#### Level 2 — STANDARD *(recommended)*
**Purpose:** find the recognition gap ward by ward, and direct against it weekly.
* **Firefly team:** the full 3-person core, plus surge roles activated by phase or KPI
* **Presence audit:** refreshed monthly, against the same ninety-day rolling window
* **Reach reporting:** **all 40 wards**
* **Weekly brief:** yes, plus the mid-week reach check
* **Creative testing:** weekly cycle
* **Team upskilling:** the standard, plus paired brief-writing to Month 3 (Section 4.6.6)
* **Offline layer:** **SMS and USSD active across all 40 wards**; shared code, all networks
* **Crisis:** dedicated lead from Phase 2; quarterly red-team drills
* **Trade-offs:** no premium social listening licences

#### Level 3 — PREMIUM
**Purpose:** instrument everything, and refresh the read faster than the field can move.
* **Firefly team:** 3-person core + full surge bench against agreed triggers
* **Presence audit:** refreshed fortnightly
* **Reach reporting:** all 40 wards + diaspora geographies
* **Creative testing:** weekly
* **Team upskilling:** the standard, plus embedded coaching through the cycle
* **Offline layer:** everything in Standard, plus a dedicated USSD short code and expanded
WhatsApp Business API capacity
* **Analytics:** licensed social listening (Brandwatch/Meltwater class); full attribution
* **Crisis:** monthly red-team drills; retained deepfake detection capability
* **Trade-offs:** it refreshes the analysis faster than a nomination sprint has capacity to act on,
and that is a real drawback rather than a disclaimer
