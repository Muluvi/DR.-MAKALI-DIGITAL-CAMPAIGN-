Firefly commits to a scope, a method, a cadence and a standard. It does not commit to an electoral outcome, and no figure in this document should be read as one. This chapter sets out what Firefly commits to and what the campaign provides in return.

## 22.1 What Firefly commits to

**What Firefly does not ask you to believe.** The commitment is to how the work is done, not to
what the ballot returns.

## 22.2 What the campaign provides

```lead
Nine dependencies determine delivery, and three of them gate everything else: a named counterpart, read-only analytics access and the appointment of the data-protection reviewer.
```

This section lists what the proposal needs from the campaign; Chapter 23 states the decision requested. Nine dependencies determine delivery, and three of them gate everything else: a named counterpart, read-only analytics access and the appointment of the data-protection reviewer. Stated plainly:

```figure
id: fig-6-1-dependencies
```

```textversion
Nine dependencies. Three of them gate everything else:

*   **A named counterpart** with authority to approve content.
*   **Read-only analytics access** on the existing accounts — Analyst role, no publishing rights.
    Without it there is nothing to analyse, and the engagement cannot start.
*   **Appointment of the data-protection reviewer** (Section 19.2) — the long-lead item, and the
    one that can delay the offline layer if it slips.

The remaining six: about **three hours** of candidate time per week; daily ground-team photo
and video uploads; current ward-level registration data from the IEBC published file; access to
the existing NG-CDF project record; sign-off on the engagement level; and the Wiper Patriotic Front's 2027 nomination
rules or NEC resolution as soon as the party issues them (Section 5.2).
```
