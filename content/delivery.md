## 4.2 The first weeks: audit, foundations, instrumentation

### 4.2.1 The first four weeks

**Objective:** close the recognition gap outside Kitui Central ahead of the Wiper Patriotic Front's
flagbearer decision <window-chip></window-chip>.

**The strategic hypothesis being tested:** Dr. Mulu's deficit is a
geography-of-recognition problem concentrated outside Kitui Central, not a
credibility problem. If the Week 1 audit and the ward-level record confirm
this, the response is reach into Mwingi and the arid belt. If they refute it —
if the deficit is about perceived distance from ordinary voters rather than
unfamiliarity — the response is different, and the campaign will know **before**
significant effort is committed. This is the discipline the rest of the
proposal claims to embody, applied to its own first assumption.

**The PhD is the one to fix first.** The site argues that his economics doctorate and his evaluation
record are the differentiators no rival holds. Neither appears on the profile where a voter would
look for them.

**Is the Facebook presence a Page, or a personal profile in professional mode?**

A visible friends list suggests the latter. The distinction is not cosmetic. A personal profile has
no Page Insights, no Business Suite export, no delegated admin roles and no ad account — which means
most of what follows does not exist, and the strategy has a migration question attached to it before
anything else is decided.

## 4.4 Coalitions and endorsements

```lead
Coalitions are built around **shared economic and civic interests** — water, credit, market access, employment, transparency. They are never built around ethnic, clan or religious bloc identity.
```
#### The principle behind the sequence

Coalitions are built around **shared economic and civic interests** — water,
credit, market access, employment, transparency. They are never built around
ethnic, clan or religious bloc identity. Faith and community networks are
engaged as **civic institutions with legitimate service-delivery concerns**,
which is both the ethical position and the effective one: a water commitment
made to a congregation is a policy conversation, while a bloc appeal is a
liability.
