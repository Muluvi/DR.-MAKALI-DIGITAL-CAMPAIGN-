
**Dr. Makali Mulu is the Economist Governor Kitui needs**: not a politician who merely promises, but an expert economist who can show his working. Kitui does not lack money; it lacks the discipline to use it well, and that discipline is his profession. This chapter states the claim, the paradox it answers, and the answer to the one attack it invites.

## The central claim


**Why this claim.** Both Wiper rivals have already won a countywide election (see [The field in both stages](#race-the-field-in-both-stages)). What distinguishes Dr. Mulu is not a vote tally but his record (see [The public record](#record-the-public-record)). So the campaign's core narrative is that **Dr. Makali Mulu is the Economist Governor Kitui needs**: an economist who can show his working and hold others to account. It rests on one premise: Kitui voters are ready for a leader who understands economics, respects public money, communicates with clarity and respect — and is willing to be measured.

**What makes it his.** Dr. Mulu is not only an economist. He is a specialist in **verifying whether public expenditure delivers what it promised**, and the county's own audit record documents the gap: unconfirmed balances, pending bills and low development absorption (see [How county money is spent](#needs-how-county-money-is-spent)). This plan turns that qualification into a public service-delivery tracker (see [Workstream 1 — Owned platforms and the service-delivery tracker](#workstreams-workstream-1-owned-platforms-and-the-service-delivery-tracke)): a line on a CV becomes a daily, public demonstration of accountability.

**The governing line:** *"Kitui has resources. What it has lacked is leadership that understands how to use them — and the discipline to prove it did."* During the nomination contest the campaign uses its second half only (see [Discipline and persuasion principles](#message-discipline-and-persuasion-principles)).

## The resource paradox

**Kitui has ample developmental resources; what it lacks is disciplined, accountable deployment.** The county's Fiscal Strategy Paper for FY2026/27 gives the executive an annual envelope of **KES {{budget.total|bn}} billion** ([The county's budget and plan](#needs-the-county-s-budget-and-plan)). Against it, the Auditor-General's FY2023/24 report records unconfirmed cash balances of KES {{audit.cash|m}} million, KES {{audit.uncollected|m}} million in uncollected revenue, an IFMIS variance and unexplained transfers, and the Controller of Budget records about KES {{budget.pending-bills.2021|bn}} billion in pending bills as of FY2020/21. [How county money is spent](#needs-how-county-money-is-spent) sets out the figures.

The county's primary developmental obstacle is not a lack of funding, but a lack of fiscal discipline, project oversight, and structural monitoring and evaluation: the competencies that define Dr. Mulu's career.
