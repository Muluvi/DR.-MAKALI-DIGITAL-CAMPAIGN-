Dr. Makali Mulu has the credentials, the experience and the vision to be an
outstanding governor of Kitui County. He also has something no rival in this
race has: a professional qualification in verifying whether public money
delivered what it promised. Unconfirmed balances, pending bills and low development absorption are what the
Auditor-General and the Controller of Budget report for the county (Section 3.7), and that qualification is currently the campaign's most under-used
asset.

**But the timeline is not the one the planning cycle assumes.** <window-chip></window-chip> Half the register, {{pool.share}}%, lives in the four
constituencies where he has never held office. The working hypothesis is that the gap there is one
of recognition rather than credibility (Section 7.1). If Week 1 confirms it, it is the kind of gap
that closes fastest through disciplined reach into Mwingi and the arid south.

This proposal sets out the layer that turns an active channel into a directed one: an audit of
what the existing accounts are actually doing (Section 14.1), a strategy derived from it, a weekly
brief that carries it to the team already publishing, an SMS and USSD layer built for the
{{ict.offline}}% of Kitui residents who are offline, a published ethics charter that turns
careful data practice into a differentiator (Section 19.3), a data-protection gate that protects
the candidate from the legal exposure others in this cycle will walk into (Section 19.2), and a
public service-delivery tracker that puts his strongest credential to work before a single vote is
cast.

Firefly would like one week and one export. Everything after that is evidence.

## 23.1 The window, not the deadline

> **What sets the pace.** <window-chip></window-chip> That date is not in the campaign's gift, and every other date in this
> document is derived from it rather than chosen.
>
> **What the work needs in front of it.** Roughly six weeks for a first directed cycle, and the
> first of those six for the presence audit that sets its targets. Counting back from a window
> that opens in late October is the arithmetic that matters; a fixed decision date printed in a
> proposal only ages.
>
> **What is lost by waiting.** Each week of delay is a week less of directed output in front of the
> window, and a week of the audit's ninety-day window filling with output nobody briefed.
