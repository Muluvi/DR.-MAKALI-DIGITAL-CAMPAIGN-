## 8.0 The fourteen workstreams, and what is outside them

Sections 8.1 to 8.15 set each workstream out in full, with its own boundaries and KPIs. This page exists so the whole offer can be seen at once before any of it is read in detail.

**Workstream 9 is the one that decides the race.** Approximately **86.4%** of Kitui residents are outside the internet-using population (KNBS 2019, Tier 1), and Section 3.6.3 puts the reach of a purely digital operation at about **36.2%** of the ~200,000-vote threshold. Every other workstream is built on the assumption that this one works.

### 8.0.2 What is outside this scope

Stated as plainly as what is inside, because a boundary a proposal will not draw is a boundary the campaign discovers later:

*   **Physical event logistics** — staging, PA systems, tents, transport and fuel. Section 14 names these as subcontracted vendor classes managed by the campaign.
*   **Campaign finance administration** — expenditure returns, IEBC financial compliance filing and the reconciliation behind them.
*   **Legal representation** — election petitions, litigation, and the data-protection and electoral-law review itself. Section 12.5.5 has the campaign appoint that reviewer; Firefly builds to their sign-off rather than substituting for it.
*   **Candidate scheduling** — tour management, diary and constituency office operations.
*   **Party-level negotiation** with Wiper organs, branch committees and the national executive.
*   **Polling agent recruitment, accreditation and payment.** Firefly builds the tracking behind indicator GE-03; the campaign supplies and accredits the agents.
*   **Commissioning the nomination tracking poll.** Section 3.1.3 specifies the instrument the campaign should insist on; commissioning it is the campaign's own act, and deliberately so — a pollster paid by the digital vendor measuring the digital vendor's effect is not evidence.
*   **`[CAMPAIGN DECISION REQUIRED]`** — anything further the campaign wishes placed outside scope before contracting. This line exists to be filled in, not to be left.

### 8.0.3 How the levels change what runs

The three scope levels in Section 10.1 change the size of the team, the number of wards carrying an active SMS and USSD layer, the depth of the analytics, and the frequency of research. They do not change the list above, and they never change Workstream 4 — accessibility applies in full at every level and is not a line to trade away (Section 10.1.1).

**Firefly recommends Level 2 (Standard).** Level 1 concentrates effort where Dr. Mulu is already strongest and leaves the recognition deficit — the one thing this campaign cannot afford to leave — untouched. Level 3 asks the campaign to absorb a volume of output a nomination sprint has no capacity to review.
