## 2.2 Results from 2013 to 2022

```lead
Julius Malombe won 2022 with **198,004 votes** on a certified turnout of **61.7%** (IEBC Form 37C); Charity Ngilu won 2017 with **169,990 votes**.
```

*   **Governor, 2013:** a **named data gap**. No certified result is in hand, and the plan does not use recollection in its place. The record that closes it is the IEBC's 2013 declaration and its Kenya Gazette notice.
*   **Governor, 2017:** Charity Ngilu (NARC) won with **169,990 votes**, in an election the Court of Appeal confirmed (*Malombe v Ngilu* [2018] KECA 460). David Musila was the certified runner-up with **114,827 votes**, and the incumbent, Julius Malombe, came third with **74,681 votes**.
*   **Governor, 2022:** Julius Malombe (Wiper Democratic Movement, renamed the Wiper Patriotic Front in 2025) won with **198,004 votes** on a certified turnout of **61.7%**, 328,760 valid votes of 532,758 registered (IEBC Form 37C). David Musila (Jubilee) was runner-up with **117,606 votes**; The Star's early total of 114,606 is a media variant, and the certified share (35.77%, calculated) matches 117,606. Jonathan Mueke (UDA) came third with **10,639 votes** (reported).
*   **President, 2022 (Kitui):** Raila Odinga won **235,400** votes (**70.82%**) and William Ruto **89,419** (**26.90%**); George Wajackoyah won 2,838 and David Mwaure 1,446, with 3,306 ballots rejected (Equal Politics aggregation of IEBC results). The official record is IEBC Forms 34B and 34C, not yet in hand.
*   **Kitui Central MP, 2022:** Dr. Makali Mulu was re-elected on the Wiper ticket with **30,954 votes** (IEBC declaration as gazetted); Boniface Musambi was second with 9,866. Early media totals differ from the gazetted figure and are not used. The figure reached this plan through a press reproduction of the declaration; the Kenya Gazette notice itself is the record to hold.
*   **Senator and Woman Representative, 2022:** Senator Enoch Wambua (Wiper) won with **191,317 votes** (reported), and Woman Representative Irene Kasalu (Wiper) with **201,899 votes** (reported). Both totals are media reports. The certified declarations (IEBC Form 38C for the Senate, Form 39C for the Woman Representative) are the official record and are not yet in hand; until they are, both figures stay as reported.
*   **Ward and polling-station results:** the constituency and polling-station forms for the 2017 and 2022 governor races (IEBC Forms 37B and 37A) are public records, not yet in hand. [Where he is known, and where not](/#known-sec-6-2) needs them to test where each candidate's vote came from, ward by ward.

#### The Mwingi bloc: necessary, not sufficient

**Mwingi North, Mwingi West and Mwingi Central together command 200,198 registered voters.** That is more *registered voters* than the 2022 winning tally (198,004 votes), but registration is not turnout. At the certified 61.7% turnout the bloc casts roughly **123,522 ballots**, so even every vote there leaves the campaign about 76,478 short of 200,000 (calculated). Mwingi is therefore **necessary and not sufficient**: no path to 200,000 runs around it, and none ends there. So SMS, USSD and digital outreach go into Mwingi first, to establish Dr. Mulu as the leading alternative there.

#### The four constituencies that decide it

1.  **The Big Four (55.60% of the county):** Kitui Central, Kitui South, Mwingi Central and Mwingi North hold **296,196 registered voters** across 22 wards (calculated from IEBC's 2022 register). The candidate who leads in these four, or holds level in them, is in reach of the governorship.
2.  **Mwingi North (68,829 voters):** fourth by voters, but it holds the county's largest ward (**Kyuso, 19,921 voters**), the political home of the Wiper Patriotic Front leader, Hon. Kalonzo Musyoka (**Tseikuru, 16,471 voters**), and **Mumoni (15,877 voters)**, all from the IEBC. It is both a large electorate and the party's heartland.
3.  **Kitui South (75,372 voters):** the second-largest constituency, anchored by the dense wards of Athi (15,843) and Ikanga/Kyatune (15,384).
