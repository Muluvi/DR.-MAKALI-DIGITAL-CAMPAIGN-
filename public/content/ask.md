Dr. Makali Mulu has the credentials, the experience and the vision to be an
outstanding governor of Kitui County. He also has something no rival in this
race has: a professional qualification in verifying whether public money
delivered what it promised. Unconfirmed balances, pending bills and low development absorption are what the
Auditor-General and the Controller of Budget report for the county (Section 3.7), and that qualification is currently the campaign's most under-used
asset.

**But the timeline is not the one the planning cycle assumes.** <window-chip></window-chip> Just over half the register, 51.7%, lives in the four
constituencies where he has never held office. The working hypothesis is that the gap there is one
of recognition rather than credibility (Section 7.1). If Week 1 confirms it, it is the kind of gap
that closes fastest through disciplined reach into Mwingi and the arid south.

This proposal sets out the platform that lets his own team turn an active channel into a directed one: an audit of
what the existing accounts are actually doing (Section 14.1), a strategy derived from it, a weekly
brief the team writes and carries to the people already publishing, an SMS and USSD layer built for the
73.8% of Kitui residents who are offline, a published ethics charter that turns
careful data practice into a differentiator (Section 19.3), a data-protection gate that protects
the candidate from the legal exposure others in this cycle will walk into (Section 19.2), and a
public service-delivery tracker that puts his strongest credential to work before a single vote is
cast.

Firefly asks him to approve the first phase, Discovery, and nothing beyond it; everything after it follows proof. Discovery ends in an executed audit of the platform, every user mapped to a role, the team's phones and connectivity checked, and the legal position scoped, with the campaign's sponsor signing the requirements. Five phases follow, and each starts only when the one before it has been accepted: readiness, a pilot with the centre and two constituencies, county rollout, optimisation and election-day readiness. The campaign can stop after any of them and keep what it has. If it stops after the first, it keeps the audit, the plan and the public tools, which are open today. The scope level in Section 21.1 is set once Discovery is complete, not in this decision; his team then enters its own ninety-day export, and everything after that is evidence for the team to act on.
