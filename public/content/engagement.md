## 13.2 Who owns what: Firefly, the existing team, the campaign

```lead
**Firefly publishes nothing on Dr. Mulu's accounts and holds no credentials to them.** The only channel it operates directly is the offline layer it already holds: a WhatsApp Business API line and a USSD shortcode.
```

**The boundary.** The digital operation separates operational software, messaging infrastructure and data science, which Firefly provides, from voter mobilisation, ground staffing, broadcast advertising and logistics, which the campaign owns and runs. **Firefly publishes nothing on Dr. Mulu's accounts and holds no credentials to them.** The only channel it operates directly is the offline layer it already holds: a WhatsApp Business API line and a USSD shortcode. The access it needs to the owned accounts is read-only, analytics only, so the work can be measured. Everything else on the owned channels stays with the people who run it now.

**The Firefly proposition.** Firefly is proposed as the campaign's **intelligence and strategy layer**, not as a replacement for the team already publishing. The value sits above the output: an audit of what the existing channels are doing, a strategy derived from it, a weekly brief that turns the strategy into instructions, a measurement cycle that says whether it worked, and one operated channel, the offline SMS, USSD and WhatsApp layer, reaching the voters no owned account can.

What matters to a decision is who decides what, which is Section 13.4. This annex now carries only the rhythm the engagement itself creates; the campaign's own internal forums — the candidate's Monday war room, the Wednesday vendor review, the monthly board — are not Firefly's to schedule. The table is the engagement's rhythm in full.

<!-- keep -->
| Cadence | Meeting | Participants | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily (15 min)** | Stand-up | Firefly core | Priorities, escalations, live issues |
| **Daily (async)** | Ground asset upload | Ground team → shared repository | Raw photo/video edited and published within hours |
| **Weekly (60 min)** | Strategy sync | Firefly Director + Campaign Comms Director | Prior-week performance; next-week calendar approved; itinerary alignment |
| **Weekly** | Creative review | Content Lead + Kikamba Producer | Language and cultural sign-off before scheduling |
| **Fortnightly** | Ward champion call | Volunteer Coordinator + ward champions | Ground sentiment; content distribution; leaderboard |
| **Monthly** | Performance review | Firefly Director + Campaign leadership + `[campaign counsel, where voter-file work is live]` | Analytics report; competitive brief |
| **Monthly** | Consented-list review | Firefly Director + Volunteer Coordinator | Consented-list growth against the phase target (Chapter 14); opt-out rate; ward coverage |
| **Quarterly** | Red-team simulation | Full team + external panel | Crisis drill report and matrix revision (Annex F.6) |

"Firefly Director" in this table is the Strategy Director of Section 17.2.
