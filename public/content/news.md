## 4.3 What each channel can physically reach

#### Platform Sizing & Realistic In-County Reach

The digital footprint in Kitui County is concentrated in urban nodes (Kitui Town, Mwingi Town, Kwa Vonza university corridor) and among educated youth. In-county active users per platform are not published; Section 4.3 sizes what digital can reach from the published rates alone.

```lead
If the campaign reached 100% of every internet-connected voter in Kitui County, it would reach about **158,696 voters** (modelled), and even if every one of them voted for him he would be **41,304 votes short** of the 200,000 benchmark.
```

According to the official **CA/KNBS ICT report on the 2023/24 Kenya Housing Survey** (Tier 1), **26.2% of Kitui's population uses the internet** (up from 13.6% in the 2019 KNBS Census), while **73.8% of the population resides in an offline media environment**, with mobile phone ownership at **44.1%**.

```figure
id: fig-3-8-ceiling
```

> [!CRITICAL]
> **STRATEGIC REALITY CHECK: THE DIGITAL CEILING**
>
> If the campaign reached **100% of every internet-connected voter in Kitui County**, it would reach about **158,696 voters** (modelled: 26.2% internet use applied to the July 2026 register). Reach is not votes, and even if every one of them voted for him he would be **41,304 votes short** of the 200,000 benchmark.
>
> Therefore, digital channels alone **cannot mathematically win this election**. Victory depends on dominating the offline media and field infrastructure (Section 15.3.3), while using digital channels (Section 15.3.2) with surgical precision to shape elite opinion, organize youth volunteers, and mobilize the out-of-county diaspora.

**The SMS and USSD layer reaches more voters than the resident rate implies.** On the resident basis (44.1% of all residents) about 267,115 voters own a phone. On the adult basis (75.7% of adults, KDHS 2022, weighted by the register's 52% women) about **459,000** do and about 147,000 do not (both modelled on the July 2026 register as reported, so Tier 3). That is roughly 1.7 times as many voters within reach of SMS and USSD. The digital ceiling above does not move: internet use among adults aged 15–49 is 34.7% for men and 23.3% for women.
