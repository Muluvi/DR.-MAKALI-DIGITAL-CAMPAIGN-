
The campaign's objectives are structured across two separate timelines, each with its own clocks, success parameters, and strictly traceable targets. Every objective traces directly either to securing the Wiper nomination poll share or delivering the ~200,000 vote threshold at the general election. All non-traceable, purely digital vanity or procedural metrics have been eliminated.

## Objectives for the nomination window

*   **Active Period (Clock):** 29 August 2026 to 15 November 2026.
*   **Success Definition:** Erase the current 15.3-point deficit and secure the Wiper Democratic Movement gubernatorial nomination by achieving a measured countywide public preference share of **40.0%+** in the official, party-commissioned surveys (Tier 2).

### Commitment 1 — Closing the Sub-County Recognition Gap
*   **Traceability:** Traces directly to Wiper nomination poll share by elevating name recognition in the northern Mwingi and arid southern sub-counties where Dr. Mulu's constituency-bounded profile is weakest (Tier 1 constraint).
*   **Baseline Figure:** 22.1% countywide poll preference share (Mizani Survey, 7 August 2026, Tier 2). Sub-county recognition in Mwingi is a **named data gap** — no published poll reports it, and the campaign does not estimate it. It is established by the Week 1 baseline instrument before this commitment is scored.
*   **Target Figure:** 40.0%+ countywide poll preference share. The Mwingi recognition target is set against the Week 1 measured baseline once it exists, not against an assumed starting point.
*   **Deadline:** 31 October 2026.
*   **Named Owner:** Director of Digital Communications & Media.
*   **Escalation Trigger:** If bi-weekly internal tracking surveys show regional recognition flat or falling across two consecutive waves, trigger immediate reallocation of paid-media weight to localised Kikamba voice-note broadcast and USSD push messaging.

### Commitment 2 — Targeted Opinion-Poll Female Demographic Preference Lift
*   **Traceability:** Traces directly to nomination poll share by targeting the county's largest demographic (women are 587,151 of 1,136,187 residents, 51.7%, Tier 1; the register is not published by sex) and addressing household economic security.
*   **Baseline Figure:** **Named data gap.** The published Mizani rounds report countywide aggregates only; no preference share by sex or age band exists in the public domain. Established by the Week 1 baseline instrument.
*   **Target Figure:** Set against the Week 1 measured baseline, as a stated point gain rather than an absolute share assumed in advance.
*   **Deadline:** 31 October 2026.
*   **Named Owner:** Demographic Outreach & Persuasion Lead.
*   **Escalation Trigger:** If mid-October focus groups find weak or negative policy association with Dr. Mulu's economic agenda among women in the target wards, immediately deploy targeted direct-to-WhatsApp audio explainers centering household poultry subsidies and water proximity financing.

## Objectives for the general election

*   **Active Period (Clock):** 16 November 2026 to 10 August 2027.
*   **Success Definition:** Mobilize and deliver a minimum of **200,000 verified votes** (Tier 1) at the ballot box, surpassing Governor Malombe's 2022 winning threshold (198,004 votes, Tier 1).

### Commitment 3 — Decentralized Ward-Level Voter Turnout Database
*   **Traceability:** Traces directly to the ~200,000 vote victory threshold by establishing a verified database of supporters mapped to individual polling stations to drive turnout (Tier 1).
*   **Baseline Figure:** 0 registered, verified supporters in the campaign’s centralized database.
*   **Target Figure:** 200,000 registered, verified supporters in the central database (Tier 1).
*   **Deadline:** 31 May 2027.
*   **Named Owner:** Director of Field Operations & Database Management.
*   **Escalation Trigger:** If database registration milestones drop below 15% of the monthly target trajectory (e.g., failing to reach 80,000 by 15 February 2027), activate localized SMS outreach and initiate physical sign-up networks through mobile-money agents.

### Commitment 4 — Offline Rural Electorate Network (SMS/USSD Enrollment)
*   **Traceability:** Traces directly to the ~200,000 vote threshold by establishing direct communication with the 86.4% offline population in rural wards (Tier 1 constraint).
*   **Baseline Figure:** 0 active offline rural subscribers.
*   **Target Figure:** 120,000 active, verified SMS/USSD offline subscribers across non-urban wards (Tier 1).
*   **Deadline:** 30 June 2027.
*   **Named Owner:** SMS Infrastructure & Offline Network Engineer.
*   **Escalation Trigger:** If weekly offline subscriber enrollment falls below 5,000 new entries, deploy physical QR and USSD sign-up sheets to agricultural markets, cattle auctions, and water distribution points.

### Commitment 5 — Ward-Level Captain Mobilization Network
*   **Traceability:** Traces directly to the ~200,000 vote threshold by creating the local physical structure required to run get-out-the-vote (GOTV) operations (Tier 1).
*   **Baseline Figure:** 0 active Ward Captains.
*   **Target Figure:** 400 active, trained Ward Captains (exactly 10 captains per ward across all 40 wards) (Tier 1).
*   **Deadline:** 31 January 2027.
*   **Named Owner:** Grassroots Operations Coordinator.
*   **Escalation Trigger:** If any of the 40 wards has fewer than 8 verified and active captains by 15 January 2027, automatically trigger targeted SMS recruitment and deploy regional organizers to host physical briefing forums.
