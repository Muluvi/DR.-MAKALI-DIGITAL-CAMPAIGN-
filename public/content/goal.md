## 8.1 The goal and the two deadlines

```lead
The campaign operates against two deadlines: the Wiper nomination <window-chip></window-chip> and the 10 August 2027 general election.
```

The campaign operates against two deadlines: the Wiper nomination <window-chip></window-chip> and the 10 August 2027 general election. Each has its own clock, its own measure of success and its own targets. Every objective traces either to winning the Wiper Patriotic Front nomination or to delivering the ~200,000 vote threshold at the general election; digital measures that trace to neither are left out (Section 18.4).

*   **The clock:** from signature to the close of the party's evaluation window in late 2026, then to the 10 August 2027 general election.
*   **Success:** the Wiper Patriotic Front gubernatorial nomination, won by establishing verifiable name recognition and organised ground reach across all eight constituencies; then at least **200,000 votes** at the ballot box, the benchmark Section 2.5 sets from Governor Malombe's 2022 winning total of 198,004.

**The two deadlines:**

1.  **The nomination window, Q4 2026 (reported):** <window-chip></window-chip> Success means showing organised reach in the 275,570-voter pool (Mwingi North, Mwingi West, Mwingi Central and Kitui South), where Dr. Makali Mulu has never held office. That he is less known there is the working hypothesis Section 7.1 states and Week 1 tests, not a measured fact.
2.  **The general election, 10 August 2027:** turning out the 200,000-vote coalition a plurality win needs in a multi-candidate field.
