*   **Pending bills.** The Controller of Budget records outstanding pending bills of about **KSh 1.3 billion as of FY2020/21**.
*   **Development absorption.** The Controller of Budget's County Budget Implementation Review Report records **18%** in the first quarter of FY2025/26. Media reports of a 5% rate are not used.
*   **The envelope and the county's own frame.** The county runs a KSh 13.79 billion envelope ([the county's budget and plan](/#needs-sec-1-5), unconfirmed). Its development theme for the year is **KESTA — Accelerating Kitui Economic and Social Transformation Agenda**, with a stated priority on raising household income. The county government names the same goal Hon. Dr. Benson Makali Mulu does, so the argument is about *execution*, not direction.

#### The county's scorecard: where the itemised money went

The county's *Delivering the Kitui Promise* scorecard (August 2022 – August 2025) itemises 401 of the 2,244 projects it claims, KSh 1.36 billion in all. Summed from its own tables:

| Constituency | Projects | KSh, itemised | KSh per registered voter (2022, calculated) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Kitui Central | 69 | 254.9m | 3,278 |
| Mwingi North | 54 | 211.2m | 3,068 |
| Kitui South | 59 | 197.5m | 2,620 |
| Mwingi Central | 59 | 182.3m | 2,456 |
| Kitui East | 45 | 133.7m | 2,045 |
| Kitui Rural | 28 | 109.9m | 1,998 |
| Kitui West | 33 | 62.9m | 1,065 |
| Mwingi West | 27 | 50.5m | 884 |
| Countywide or headquarters | — | 160.9m | — |

**How to read it, and how not to.** It covers only the 401 itemised projects, not the county budget and not all 2,244 projects (a record of what the county claims, not evidence that the claims are true). Kitui Central's figure includes the county referral hospital and town works that serve the whole county: its health spending alone is KSh 76.6m of the KSh 153.4m attributed to constituencies (county scorecard). The pool's four constituencies receive 53.3% of constituency-attributed spending against 51.7% of the register, so the plan does not claim the pool was neglected. The specific gap is Mwingi West, with less than a third of Kitui Central's spending per voter and KSh 1.9m for water. The full list is [kitui-scorecard-projects.csv](/data/kitui-scorecard-projects.csv), and the tracker's questions are in [Owned platforms and the service-delivery tracker](/#workstreams-sec-15-1-1).

**What it means for the campaign.** The audit record is the evidence for Dr. Mulu's position ([the position](/#position-sec-9-1)): the county's problem is not a lack of money but how it is managed, which is the discipline he has practised.
