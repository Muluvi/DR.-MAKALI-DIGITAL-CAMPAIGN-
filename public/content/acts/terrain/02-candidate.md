# The candidate

Hon. Dr. Benson Makali Mulu, PhD (Economics), CBS, is the most technically credentialled
candidate in this race, and the credentials are the argument.

Across his tenure in the National Assembly he has built a reputation as one of Kenya's most
consistent and authoritative voices on macroeconomic governance, fiscal discipline and budget
oversight. His long service on the Budget and Appropriations Committee gives him an operational
understanding of public finance systems and county resource allocation that no other candidate
in the field can claim.

## What the record actually holds

**Macroeconomic authority.** A doctorate in economics and leadership on the Budget and
Appropriations Committee. This is the spine of the platform, and it converts directly into a
weekly public-finance explainer series in the candidate's own voice.

**Monitoring and evaluation.** A certified consultant in project management and M&E, a member
of the Evaluation Society of Kenya, and a designated M&E Champion by the Ministry of Finance.
A governor who can be held to published targets is a different proposition from one who cannot,
and this is the credential that makes an interactive service-delivery tracker credible rather
than decorative.

**International standing.** Project management and financial advisory roles with the United
Nations — the credential that carries furthest with professionals and the diaspora.

**Delivery on the ground.** KSh 47 million in educational bursaries disbursed to 12,573
constituents. Kitui Central certified as the best-evaluated constituency in the Eastern region
in FY2014/15, ranking first out of 71 constituencies in its national peer group.

**Longevity.** Seventeen years in active political leadership, thirteen of them as Member of
Parliament for Kitui Central, and the Commander of the Order of the Burning Spear.

**An existing digital footprint.** The Kitui Central NG-CDF portal is already live and already
his — an asset to build on rather than a budget line to spend twice.

The strategic problem is not the record. It is that the record is known in one constituency out
of eight.
