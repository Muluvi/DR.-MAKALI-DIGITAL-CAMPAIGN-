# Four routes, and one of them is a trap

Four ward combinations get tested against the 200,000 benchmark. Three of them clear it. The
fourth is the one the campaign would take by instinct.

## Path A — the Mwingi triad

Mwingi Central, Mwingi North and Mwingi West: 74,231 plus 68,829 plus 57,138 equals **200,198
registered voters** across fifteen wards, 37.58% of the county, and 2,194 more registered
voters than Malombe's winning total.

The turnout reality is the important part. Those 200,198 registered voters produce roughly
124,100 ballots at the 62% baseline. Even an unprecedented 80% share of them — 99,280 votes —
leaves the campaign roughly 99,000 short. Mwingi is the largest single reservoir in the county
and the campaign cannot win without a decisive margin there. It also cannot win on it alone.

## Path B — the central-south-west axis

Kitui Central, Kitui South and Kitui West: 77,764 plus 75,372 plus 59,047 equals **212,183
registered voters** across fifteen wards, 39.83% of the county, and 14,179 clear of the
threshold.

Home base, the county's largest southern constituency and the peri-urban west form a
contiguous coalition that clears 200,000 on the register.

## Path C — the twelve megawards

Kyuso, Township, Kwa Vonza/Yatta, Mutonguni, Tseikuru, Kyangwithya West, Mumoni, Athi, Kauwi,
Kyangwithya East, Ikanga/Kyatune and Mulango: **201,267 registered voters** across twelve
wards in six constituencies, 37.78% of the county.

The campaign does not need to contest all forty wards at equal intensity. Twelve wards, worked
hard, reach a register equal to the winning threshold.

## Path D — the home belt, and why it cannot work

Kitui Central, Kitui West and Kitui Rural: 77,764 plus 59,047 plus 55,000 equals **191,811
registered voters** across thirteen wards — 8,189 short of the 200,000 benchmark before a
single vote is cast.

At 62% turnout the home belt produces 118,923 ballots. If Dr. Mulu took an unprecedented 80% of
every vote cast across the entire belt — 95,138 votes — he would still finish more than 100,000
votes short.

**A home-constituency strategy is mathematically impossible.** Maximising margins in Kitui
Central, Kitui West and Kitui Rural cannot produce a governorship. Outward expansion into
Mwingi and Kitui South is not a preference or a growth ambition. It is an arithmetic
requirement.

## The constituencies that decide it

Four constituencies — Kitui Central at 77,764, Kitui South at 75,372, Mwingi Central at 74,231
and Mwingi North at 68,829 — hold **296,196 registered voters** across 22 wards, 55.60% of the
county. Parity or dominance across those four decides the election.

Mwingi North carries leverage beyond its fourth-place volume. It hosts the largest ward in the
county in Kyuso at 19,921 voters, the ancestral base of Wiper party leader Kalonzo Musyoka in
Tseikuru at 16,471, and Mumoni at 15,877. It is simultaneously a voter powerhouse and the
party's spiritual heartland — which matters disproportionately in a nomination decided inside
the party.

Kitui South, second by volume, is anchored by Athi at 15,843 and Ikanga/Kyatune at 15,384.
