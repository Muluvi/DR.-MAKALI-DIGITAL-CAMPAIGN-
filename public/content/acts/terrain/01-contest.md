# The ticket is decided by a poll, not a primary

Everything the campaign does before December rests on a single claim: that the Wiper Democratic
Movement will settle its Kitui gubernatorial ticket by countywide opinion poll rather than by a
competitive primary.

That claim is single-sourced. It comes from a campaign report, not a party communiqué, and no
Wiper official has confirmed it. It sits at the bottom of the evidence ladder, and the strategy
built on it is provisional by design — stated plainly here so that the re-check happens
deliberately rather than by accident.

Two things would move it from assumption to operating fact. One is a signed resolution of the
Wiper National Executive Council. The other is the commissioned pollster's terms of reference —
sample frame, questionnaire, fieldwork dates, and the count and weighting method. The second is
the one the campaign can actually obtain, and the more useful of the two, because it is what
tells you whether the instrument deciding the ticket is sound.

## What a sound instrument looks like

If a poll is going to choose the candidate, the campaign has to be able to audit it. Four
parameters decide whether a party-commissioned survey is worth accepting.

**The sample frame** must be a multi-stage stratified random model drawn from the official
IEBC polling station registries across all eight sub-counties, excluding non-voters and
unregistered residents.

**The sample size** must be a minimum of 1,200 to 1,500 registered voters countywide, which
yields a margin of error of ±2.53% at 95% confidence. Sub-samples must be allocated in
proportion to registered voters per sub-county — Kitui Central's share alone implies roughly
175 to 220 respondents.

**The question wording** matters more than the sample. Unaided recall — *"If the election for
Governor were held today, who would you vote for?"* — measures real intensity of support.
Aided recall, read from a list of candidates, systematically inflates anyone with passive name
recognition and thin commitment behind it. A ticket decided on aided recall is a different
contest from one decided on unaided recall, and the campaign should know which it is entering.

**The weighting** is where the argument is won or lost. Raw data must be weighted to the actual
IEBC sub-county voter distribution. A survey that over-samples Kitui Township at the expense of
Kyuso or Mutha does not describe the county.

## Why the weighting argument is worth making

Kitui Central holds 77,764 registered voters — the largest and most concentrated block in the
county, and the candidate's home ground. In an unweighted poll, concentrated high-intensity
support in one constituency is exactly the signal that gets diluted. Proper geographic
weighting is not a technicality the campaign should leave to the pollster; it is the difference
between a result that reflects the county's turnout anchor and one that does not.

## Where the numbers actually stand

There is no basis for optimism in the published polling, and the campaign should not manufacture
any. The August 2026 Mizani Africa survey puts Dr. Irene Kasalu at 37.4% and Dr. Mulu at 22.1%.

That is a flat 15.3-point countywide deficit. Two survey rounds — June and August 2026 — are not
enough to establish a trend in either direction, and nothing here should be read as one. The
campaign is trailing, and the gap has to close before the mid-November evaluation window.

## If it becomes a delegate vote instead

If Wiper reverts to a delegate-based primary, the phasing changes and the campaign triggers a
delegate whip operation instead.

The first move is to map and profile the estimated 1,200 to 1,500 official delegates —
constituency executives, sub-branch officials, ward-level committee members. The second is to
reassign the 400 ward captains from general voter outreach to peer-to-peer lobbying, each
captain working the delegates resident in their own ward. The third is a dedicated encrypted
SMS and USSD channel to those delegates, carrying policy briefs rather than slogans.

The contingency exists so that a change in mechanism costs the campaign days, not weeks.
